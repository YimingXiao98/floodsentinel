# Post-hoc paired bootstrap intervals for the pooled policy differences

Everything in this file is post hoc. Nothing was prespecified. The intervals add a sampling-uncertainty band to differences that the frozen Study 2 and Study 3 reports already state as pooled sums; they do not change any frozen verdict. Method: county-events are the resampling unit; 10,000 resamples with replacement, seed 20260918, percentile 95% intervals. For each comparison A minus B the pooled difference is the sum over the resampled county-events of A's unobserved flood-stage hours minus B's, with the same construction for episodes observed; the mean paired difference is the pooled difference divided by n, so its interval is the pooled interval rescaled. A negative value favors A (less unobserved time; for episodes, a positive value favors A). One shared index matrix per study makes every comparison within a study use the same resamples. Generated by `eval/posthoc_bootstrap_intervals.py`, which reads `eval/results/study_v4_additive`, `eval/results/study_v3_hybrid_rev2`, `eval/results/post_hoc_comparators/summary.json` and `eval/results/robustness_checks/summary.json` and writes only into `eval/results/posthoc_bootstrap`.

## Phase-free control (Study 3 county-events at 24 mission-start offsets), post hoc

What was done: the reported 56.00 / 52.63 / 103.88 h are medians across 24 offsets of each policy's pooled value; with an even number of offsets the median is the mean of two middle draws, and the middle draws differ across policies, so no single offset realizes all three medians. Instead of picking one offset, each county-event's difference (phase-free minus hybrid, and phase-free minus phase adaptation) is averaged over all 24 offsets, offsets acting as strata, and county-events are then resampled with the Study 3 index matrix. The offset-0 draw, which is the frozen Study 3 run itself, and the range of the 24 per-offset intervals are given beside the offset-averaged result. ## Reading the intervals

The intervals are wide relative to the point estimates because the pooled hours are concentrated: in Study 3 the three county-events with the most hybrid unobserved time (Sarasota, New Castle, Marshall) hold 60% of the hybrid's 60.25 h, so a resample that draws them several times or not at all moves the sum by tens of hours. The bootstrap treats county-events as exchangeable draws from a population of comparable county-events; the selection was mechanical but not random, so the bands describe sampling variability among these cases rather than a population inference. Comparator clocks enter as per-county-event offset medians, exactly as in the frozen reports, so the within-county-event spread over clock offsets is not resampled. None of this alters the frozen verdicts: Study 2 failed P2 and Study 3 failed Q1 under their prespecified rules regardless of these bands.
